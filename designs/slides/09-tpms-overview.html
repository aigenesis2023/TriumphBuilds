TPMS overview.

Tyre Pressure Monitoring System — Part of the Immobiliser
!
Safety Warning
The tyre pressure monitoring system is not to be used as a tyre pressure gauge when adjusting tyre pressures — always check with an accurate gauge when tyres are cold.
Read full warning ›
Component 01
Instruments
Used to display the tyre pressure value, the tyre symbol, and the TPMS warning light.
Component 02
Immobiliser /
TPMS ECM
Receives the data from the tyre pressure sensors and sends the information to the instrument pack.
Component 03
Pressure Sensor
Inside the front and rear wheel — measures air pressure and transmits data. Sealed unit, do not open.
